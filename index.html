Tile & grout cleaning

Professional tile and grout cleaning for high-use areas in your home.

Tile and grout can collect dirt, grease, and discoloration over time, especially in kitchens, bathrooms, and other high-use areas. Our tile and grout cleaning service focuses on removing buildup from both the tile surface and the grout lines, where standard mopping doesn’t reach.

We use a process that breaks down embedded soil and lifts it out of the grout while cleaning the tile surface at the same time. This helps improve the appearance of the floor and reduces the darkening that can happen in grout lines. Each cleaning is based on the condition of the tile and level of buildup so the results are consistent across different areas of your home.
